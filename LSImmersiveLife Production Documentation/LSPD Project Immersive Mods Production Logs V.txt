### Gameplay flow

- Rejecting foot-citizen documents can lead to flight or compliance. A compliant citizen raises their hands; **40% of compliant outcomes** then use a kneeling surrender pose. The pose name is from GTA V’s stock arrest script, which sequences a hands-up reaction into `random@arrests/kneeling_arrest_idle` ([decompiled arrest script](https://github.com/brendan-rius/gta-v-decompiled-scripts/blob/master/re_arrests.c4)). If that animation cannot load, the citizen stays hands-up. The hands-up task also has a documented native example ([Cfx example](https://docs.fivem.net/docs/cookbook/2020/01/06/using-the-new-console-key-bindings/)).
- With the current controls, **G** starts contact, **E** begins the physical arrest, and **R** requests transport. The arrest checks that the player is close and on foot, starts GTA’s arrest task, and confirms the handcuff state after the contact. Leaving before it completes cancels the arrest while leaving the citizen compliant.
- In a Police car or van, the arrested citizen transfers to Convoy as already arrested, avoiding a second arrest. On a motorcycle or without a usable transport vehicle, **R** requests the saved favorite Backup unit; **B** remains available for an explicit Backup request.
- Backup keeps ownership of its officers, vehicle, and station route. Convoy’s existing paired escort, rear-door loading, unloading, and handoff routines handle the physical custody movement. Arrested custody no longer expires or clears just because the player walks away. Once Backup’s vehicle is 150 meters away, the player can resume other Patrol activities while Backup continues the delivery.
