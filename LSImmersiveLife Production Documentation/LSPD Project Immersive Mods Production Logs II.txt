If Anyi is already in a suitable police vehicle, the patrol system should simply adopt that vehicle as the active patrol vehicle. No respawn. No teleport. No sudden replacement. The current patrol function trusts Station.SpawnPosition; then CreatePlayerPatrol() uses that coordinate and calls PlaceOnGround() afterward.  When that configured coordinate happens to be bad, elevated, interior-adjacent, or otherwise unsuitable for a vehicle spawn, PlaceOnGround() is not enough to guarantee a nice usable location. That is why your “teleported to station, then Venti and Polignus fall into the void” scene is believable.

So I would change that into a safe station patrol resolver. The station coordinate becomes a preferred point, not a sacred spawn point. The resolver tests ground, checks whether the location is sensible for a vehicle, then tries nearby offsets or a road node. That is a much more stable system.

And I especially agree with your point that current-position patrol should not create a car directly beside Venti. Case lifecycle ≠ world entity lifecycle.

A case can become Completed while the corpse, vehicle, evidence-like scene actors, or other world entities remain for a short while.

That gives you the natural police scene feeling: Venti shoots a suspect, the body remains. Backup arrives. You investigate. You stand around. You drive away. Eventually the cleanup system sees that you are far enough away and removes the scene.

The proper order is:
